## what-an-fde-is
- [T4] OpenAI defines FDEs as engineers who "lead complex end-to-end deployments of frontier models in production". They own "discovery, technical scoping, system design, build, and production rollout", and success is measured by "production adoption, measurable workflow impact, and eval-driven feedback that changes product and model roadmaps". — OpenAI, Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) - SF https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/openai/967f94aa-1706-4dba-ac89-bfbc2c38b688 (2026-09-11) · confidence: high
- [T4] OpenAI's healthcare FDE post draws an explicit line: "This role owns the technical solution; ownership of the commercial or executive relationship is not required." — OpenAI, FDE Healthcare - Seattle https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/openai/a4332746-163e-4506-b62a-90758fb7a950 (2026-08-06) · confidence: high
- [T4] Anthropic's Staff FDE post says "Customers bring you in to lead, not only to implement: you decide what should be built, and then make sure it gets built well." The role sits in the GTM organisation. — Anthropic, Staff Forward Deployed Engineer https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5449229008 (2026-10-14) · confidence: high
- [T4] Palantir describes FDSE responsibilities as "similar to those of a startup CTO: you'll work in small teams with minimal supervision and own end-to-end execution of high stakes projects". Its Forward Deployed AI Engineer post uses the same framing. — Palantir, Forward Deployed Software Engineer - US Government https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/289ad049-7b4e-41e3-8a39-146fbeb6fb64 (created 2020-04-15, still live 2026-10-17) · confidence: high
- [T4] Decagon's Engineering Lead, Strategic Deployments owns "1–3 Strategic Deals end-to-end, operating like the founding CTO of each". — Decagon https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/decagon/13056902-5ab6-4451-83a8-aa72e9f72532 (2026-07-31) · confidence: high
- [T4] Employers use many titles for this work: "Applied AI Engineer" and "Applied AI Architect" (Anthropic), "Agent Deployment Engineer" (Decagon), "Strategist, Agent Development" (Sierra), "Frontier Agents Engineer (Forward Deployed Engineering)" (Scale), "Deployment Strategist" (ElevenLabs, Mistral) and "Forward Deployed Finance Engineer" (Harvey). Title-based counts therefore undercount the role. — employer ATS feeds (2026-10-17) · confidence: high
- [T2] Bloomberry's analysis of about 1,000 FDE posts splits them into "Builder FDE" (60%), "Sales Engineer+" (30%) and "Internal Tools Builder" (10%). — Bloomberry, "I analyzed 1000+ forward deployed engineer jobs" https://bloomberry.com/blog/i-analyzed-1000-forward-deployed-engineer-jobs-what-i-learned/ (2025-11-18, updated 2026-01-25) · confidence: medium
- [T2] Salesforce's head of the function describes the FDE as "hacker meets customer value meets professional services person" and calls coding "table stakes". — Salesforce Newsroom, "Forward Deployed Engineers Are Proving AI Makes Tech Jobs More Human" https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/forward-deployed-engineers-making-ai-jobs-more-human/ (2026-03-18) · confidence: medium

## origins-and-economics
- [T2] Palantir's 2020 S-1 names the role: "Our forward deployed engineers ('FDEs') have travelled to bases in Afghanistan and factories in the industrial Midwest to deploy our platforms… As FDEs help customers make the most of our software, they observe users' challenges firsthand." — Palantir Form S-1 https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000119312520230013/d904406ds1.htm (2020-08-25) · confidence: high
- [T2] The S-1 shows the economics by phase. In 2019, Acquire-phase customers produced $0.6M revenue at a $65.4M contribution loss, Expand-phase customers $176.3M at a (43)% contribution margin, and Scale-phase customers $565.7M at 55%. The top 25% of Scale customers reached 87%. — Palantir Form S-1 (same URL) (2020-08-25) · confidence: high
- [T2] Palantir's S-1 says it runs pilot deployments "at no or low cost" and that "During these short-term pilots, we operate the accounts at a loss". It gives the sales cycle as "often… six to nine months but can extend to a year". — Palantir Form S-1 (same URL) (2020-08-25) · confidence: high
- [T4] Former Palantir FDE Nabeel Qureshi (2015–2023) calls the FDE/product-development split "one of the key 'secrets' of the company". FDEs built fast, sometimes hacky solutions on site, and product engineers then generalised them. Tools such as Magritte, Contour and Workshop started as field work. — Nabeel Qureshi, "Reflections on Palantir" https://nabeelqu.substack.com/p/reflections-on-palantir (2024-10-15) · confidence: medium
- [T4] Qureshi reports critics' view that Palantir was "a consulting company masquerading as software". He answers with its 2023 gross margin of about 80% against 32% for Accenture. These are possible echoes of company-reported figures; he does not cite where they come from. — Nabeel Qureshi (same URL) (2024-10-15) · confidence: medium
- [T4] a16z argues that services-led FDE motions "trade margin for moat". It cites gross margins at IPO of 63.2% for ServiceNow and 54.1% for Workday, rising to 79% and 75% in 2024, and counts 22 of OpenAI's 311 open roles as FDE or solutions roles at the time. — a16z (Joe Schmidt), "Trading Margin for Moat" https://a16z.com/services-led-growth/ (2025-06-04) · confidence: medium
- [T4] Sierra's Flagship Deployment strategist is told to "Drive financial success for both the customer and Sierra… manage down cost to serve over time, improving margins". This puts deployment unit economics inside the role itself. — Sierra, Strategist, Agent Development - Flagship Deployment https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/sierra/15f81581-ad9c-4b03-8ed7-097fbbce0469 (2026-09-18) · confidence: high
- [T3] OpenAI launched "The OpenAI Deployment Company" with more than $4B from 19 firms led by TPG (co-leads Advent, Bain Capital, Brookfield). It acquired the consultancy Tomoro, whose roughly 150 FDEs join the new company, and named Bain & Company, Capgemini and McKinsey as alliance partners. — ERP Today, "OpenAI Launches Deployment Company…" https://erp.today/openai-launches-deployment-company-to-bring-ai-into-enterprise-operations/ (2026-05-11) · confidence: medium
- [T3] Anthropic's implementation joint venture with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, Goldman Sachs and others is now named Ode. It has about $1.5B and acquired Fractional AI. Its roughly 100 engineers are "over half" former founders, and sources say FDE demand "far outstrips supply". — TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/anthropic-blackstone-bet-the-next-trillion-dollar-ai-business-is-implementation-not-models/ (2026-07-15) · confidence: medium
