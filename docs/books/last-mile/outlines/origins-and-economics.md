## Argument (bible §5)
Palantir proved that losing money on pilots can buy an 87%-margin customer. The labs
adopted the pattern because models need the same last mile. The 2026 move of FDE capacity
into separate deployment companies shows the services trap is still live.

## p.goal draft
> Forward deployment is a bet: lose money in the customer's building now and get it back
> as product later. Know which bet your employer is making, because that decides what your
> work is for.
