# Ch 15: Where FDEs Work (`where-fdes-work`)

Part IV · The Career · phase-num 15 · 3,500–4,500 words.

## Argument (bible §5)
The employer type changes the job more than the title does. Labs, application startups,
Palantir-style platforms and the new deployment companies offer different work, pay and exits.

## p.goal draft
> Two offers with the same title can be different jobs: one turns your field work into product,
> the other bills your hours. Choose the employer type first; it sets the work, the pay mix and the exits.

- **15.1 Where the openings are.** The famous employers are a small share. Plank census (T2, vendor
  that sells FDE services): 1,333 posts, 565 companies, two-thirds US, 9% at labs + Palantir.
  Bloomberry 58% at 11–200 staff (T2, different sample and window; not combined). Verified box:
  own feed counts 2026-10-17 (T1 count): FDE-titled of open roles at 11 employers + DeployCo's
  board; Palantir 32 of 77 are internships/new grad, 28 created >2 years ago, oldest 2009.
  Echo: 川观/霞光's "565 companies, $193K" is Plank's census relayed. Growth figures: not blended
  (Appendix D).
- **15.2 Four employer types, by what they buy with your time.** Lab buys production at flagship
  accounts + signal; application startup buys the deal and its own product's deployment; platform
  buys account expansion; deployment company sells your hours as delivered outcomes. Link Ch 2 for
  the economics; this chapter is about the week, the pay mix, the exit.
- **15.3 What the posts say the work is.** T4 for the work, primary for the ask. Lab: OpenAI
  "eval-driven feedback that changes product and model roadmaps"; Anthropic Staff "bring you in to
  lead". Startup: Decagon lead "operating like the founding CTO" of 1–3 deals, "before they threaten
  the deal", "durable improvements to the core Decagon product"; Sierra "drive revenue upside…
  manage down cost to serve"; Glean "ships to every Glean customer". Platform: Palantir new-grad and
  intern pipeline, specialised FD roles, Deployment Strategist partner. Deployment co: DeployCo
  "scaled services delivery", "standardization-versus-customization… instead of defaulting to
  bespoke work", "ramp quickly toward management", "Apply with Your Team". Onsite (Verified):
  OpenAI ~50%, 80% UAE/Japan (T3); Palantir 25% (T3) vs 3–4 days/week (Qureshi, T4) — flagged.
- **15.4 What pays: read the mix, not the midpoint.** Verified box of posted bands by type.
  Plank stage medians (T2, 388/1,333 disclose, base only); Bloomberry median (T2; not averaged with
  Plank); Bloomberry's internal OTE/quota inconsistency flagged; Harvey "75/25 Split" only.
  Reading rules: band width → level set in the offer; variable pay → commercial role; equity → a
  bet on the product loop; Palantir low base + RSUs (Qureshi "paid lower than market", T4).
- **15.5 Upside and exits.** Evidence thin: no tracking study. T3 Pragmatic Engineer (FDEs moved
