# Ch 12: Feeding the Product (`feeding-the-product`)

Part II · The Engagement · phase-num 12 · 3,500–4,500 words.

## p.goal draft
> An FDE who ships for one customer and teaches the product nothing is a contractor with better tools. The loop back to product is the job's economic reason, and it runs on a written brief.

## STEPs

**12.1 What this phase is for, and how it fails.**
- Argument: the loop is what separates an FDE from a contractor; without it the role is services revenue.
- Jarvis: "short-term lure of services revenue" (T2, video transcript re-fetched 2026-10-17).
- Failure 1: nothing comes back (Pragmatic Engineer "IT services mindset", "They don't contribute back into the platform", quit after 4 weeks; T4).
- Failure 2: generalising too early (Jarvis, T2).
- Postings: every one in the sample measures the loop (T4 as evidence of what employers ask for) -> Verified box.
- Thin evidence: no T1; reuse numbers are targets ("very much at the start of that journey").

**12.2 Composite (months 4–8).** Field log -> brief -> template; second insurer reuses roughly a fifth to a third; keeps its own held-out set. Cites Jarvis 20%->50%, Klarna lineage, Palantir FDE->PD.

**12.3 Keep a field log from week one.** Sources: eval failure categories, overrides, workarounds, things built twice. Three destinations (product / research / playbook). COBOL (T3, Ojstersek), voice -> real-time API (T3, Pragmatic Engineer).

**12.4 Separate the pattern from the customer.** Qureshi: "not worry about overfitting; PD's job..." (T4). Gravel roads / superhighways (T3, StartupHub narration, NOT McGrew's words — brief misattributes). What is theirs: data, held-out set, rules, queue codes.

**12.5 Wait for the second customer.** Jarvis "generalizing too early"; 20% -> 50% reuse target (T2). Klarna -> framework -> T-Mobile -> SDK; attribution blur (SA team per Pragmatic Engineer, #7). Verified box with product names.

**12.6 Get it adopted.** Receiver must exist: Jarvis "falling on deaf ears"; GitHub stars as internal influence. Salesforce "moved up six months" (T4). Capacity split on two axes.

**12.7 Template:** product-feedback brief, blank + filled.
