# Ch 18: Levels, Burnout and What Comes Next (`levels-burnout-and-what-comes-next`)

Part IV · The Career · phase-num 18 · 3,000–4,000 words (thin chapter; say so up front).

## Argument
FDE ladders reward leverage, not endurance. Each level up means doing less of the engagement
yourself and more through others, the roadmap and the customer's team. The FDE who tries to climb
by volume (more engagements, more travel, more systems still on their pager) produces the same
L2 evidence many times and burns out on the way. Plan level, load and exit as one decision.

## p.goal draft
> No one has published FDE attrition data, but the ladders say what gets promoted: leverage, not
> hours on site. Climb by multiplying your work, cap the three loads, and keep two exits open.
