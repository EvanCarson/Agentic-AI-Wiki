# Ch 17: Leading FDEs (`leading-fdes`)

Part IV · The Career · phase-num 17 · 3,000–4,000 words (thin evidence; say so up front).

## Argument
The bible's §5 entry gives cases but no argument sentence; this one is derived from the thesis,
the spec line ("running several accounts, hiring, team design, and metrics") and what the two
manager posts say they will measure:

Leading FDEs means running a portfolio of engagements, not a team of engineers. The manager
decides which customers get FDEs, watches every account's load-bearing commitments in one weekly
view, and measures the team on thresholds crossed, handoffs that hold and signal that reaches
product. Utilisation is the metric that turns an FDE team into a services firm.

- **17.2 The job, as the posts write it.** Anthropic first FDE Manager: player-coach "with a
  strong bias toward leadership"; staffs and oversees engagements; qualifies pre-sales; owns
  playbooks, starter repos, OKRs tied to customer outcomes and product adoption; "engagement
  reviews" as a cadence; pairs with Engagement Managers. OpenAI: success "measured by how
  consistently your team ships, how clearly you deliver signal to Research and Product, and how
  durable your team and delivery model prove to be"; "Define how we staff". Anthropic Staff FDE
  as the IC alternative: "quality bar for work you don't build yourself". Read: three jobs —

- **17.3 Capacity is strategy: choose the accounts.** Jarvis two axes (product-hypothesis
  design partners; research-valuable industries) and "be prepared to say no ... a lot of money
  to do something that's not strategic". Third filter from Part II: does the account have the
  conditions (Ch 4 pilot terms, Ch 13 five conditions)? Link Ch 2 economics. Qualify before
  staffing, not after.
- **17.4 Team shape and load.** Pods: Salesforce 1 strategist + 2 FDEs, full-time on one client
  about three months, many FDEs solo (T4); Glean pod with Forward Deployed PMs (T4); Anthropic
  Engagement Managers (T4); Cresta FDE + FDPM, 2–3 projects each, maybe 5–6 with tools (T4,
  single interview); Ramp ~15 FDEs in pods embedded in product engineering (T3). Bloomberry
  org placement 45/38/14 (T2, overlapping). Load rule (opinion): count phases, not accounts —
  at most one heavy phase per FDE. Verified box with the numbers.
- **17.5 Hiring and onboarding.** Hire on behaviour with the Ch 3 rubric; link Ch 16. Salesforce
  40–50% internal moves, six-week onboarding (2 training + 4 on the job) (T2). Cresta: no
  juniors, into a project within 2–3 weeks (T4). Sceptic: Pragmatic Engineer hire who quit after
  4 weeks over an "IT services mindset" (T4) — a manager's failure of honesty in the pitch.
  Tell candidates the shape of the next six months.
- **17.6 Measure what the thesis measures.** Not utilisation, not hours billed. a16z: quota on
  implementation leads "can also lead to counterproductive behavior"; "clean information
  sharing between the front lines and product" (T4). Proposed measures: thresholds agreed and
  crossed; handoff drills passed and systems still running months later; early calls;
  briefs that got a decision; time to first demo falling as reuse rises. Bible opinion, not tested.
