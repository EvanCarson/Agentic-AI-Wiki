## p.goal draft

> Most engagements are won or lost before the first FDE arrives, in the terms of the pilot. A free, open-ended proof of concept is built never to reach production. A short paid pilot with one KPI the sponsor owns is built to.

## STEPs

**4.1 Why the terms decide the outcome**
- The Palantir S-1 shows pilots run "at no or low cost… at a loss" and 6–9-month sales cycles (T2).
- The model the AI labs inherited: the pilot is an investment, not a favour.
- Free PoCs carry no sponsor cost, so nobody owns success.
- Link the evidence on the free-PoC → no-production pattern to Ch 13 by name (it is planned, so name it without a link).

**4.2 Proof, not proofs of concept**
- Palantir's AIP bootcamps compressed 1–3-month pilots into five days on real data. They grew to 560+ bootcamps at 465 organisations. Cite the $26M-ACV five-weeks-later and $96M examples (T2, vendor earnings calls).
- No conversion rate was ever disclosed, so say what this does and doesn't prove.
- The lesson: real data and a real workflow early, not the bootcamp format itself.

**4.3 Terms that make a pilot real**
- Synthesise Pear ("pilots should never be free", ~1 month, never over 3, a 20–50% discount on prorated ACV agreed in advance), Heavybit (one KPI, 30 days, inside a full-year frame) and Shimoni (around $10K tied to the labour budget). All T4.
- Mention Lemkin's "70%+" conversion as a low-confidence practitioner claim only.
- Note the gap: there is no independent pilot-conversion data.
- Add a Verified box for the price examples.

**4.4 The FDE's job in pre-sales**
- Anthropic's FDE Manager qualifies engagements and informs SOWs. Harvey FDEs "lead discovery, demos, pilots" on OTE (T4). Sierra's engineer scopes in pre-sales.
- The FDE is the person who can tell sales "this pilot cannot succeed as written".
- How to say it: by naming the missing term, not by refusing.

**4.5 The composite: reshaping the pilot**
- Composite box, beat 1 (bible §6): the free 3-month "customer chatbot" PoC becomes a 30-day paid pilot with one KPI, data access by day 5 and credit on conversion.
- The COO signs because the KPI is hers.
- Cite the patterns: Pear/Heavybit terms; Palantir "proof, not proofs of concept".

**4.6 The template: engagement brief and pilot terms**
- Show it blank, then filled from the composite, using ranges.
- Fields:
  - sponsor;
  - decision-maker;
  - budget owner;
  - problem hypothesis (to be tested in discovery);
  - the one KPI and its baseline source;
  - duration;
  - price and credit on conversion;
  - data-access date;
  - security-review start date;
  - exit criteria;
  - what happens on success (contract shape);
  - named customer experts and their hours per week.

## Failure signals
- No named sponsor.
- The KPI is "explore what AI can do".
- No data before week 2.
- Open-ended duration.
- The FDE first sees the terms after signature.

## Ready-to-move-on checklist (`<ul>`)
- [ ] A named sponsor with budget.
- [ ] One KPI with a baseline source.
- [ ] Duration of 90 days or less.
- [ ] A data-access date.
- [ ] Exit criteria in writing.
- [ ] Customer experts' time committed.
- [ ] Price, or an explicit reason it's free.

## Closing tip
If you can't fill the brief, the engagement isn't ready. Hand it back to sales with the blank fields named.
