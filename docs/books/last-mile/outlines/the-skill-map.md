# Ch 3: The Skill Map (`the-skill-map`)

Part I · The Role / 第 I 部分 · 角色 · phase-num 3 · 3,000–4,500 words. Thin evidence, said up front.

## p.goal draft
> Job posts measure the skills that fit on a CV, but engagements are won by four kinds of behaviour, and the posts measure only two of them well. Place yourself by what you have done.

## STEPs

**3.1 What employers ask for** (job posts as primary evidence of asks, T4 for how the job is done).
- OpenAI FDE SF (2026-09-11): 5+ years "that includes customer-facing work", Python/JavaScript, LLM systems.
- Anthropic FDE London (2026-09-14): 4+ years customer-facing; "Production experience with LLMs including... evaluation frameworks".
- Bloomberry ~1,000 posts (T2): Python 66%, TypeScript 35%, AWS 32%, agents 35%, LLM 31%, RAG 12%, customer-facing 47%; prior roles of 100 profiles.
- Verified box: spread (Mistral PhD + fine-tuning; Harvey buyout experience, Python "a plus"; ElevenLabs student clubs) + China job families (Tencent product, ByteDance R&D, interns sales-support; T3 via iFanr/36Kr).
- Thin: no data on which skills predict success.

**3.2 Four kinds of behaviour.** Technical, customer, product, judgment — each tied to a stretch of the thesis' distance and to its Part II chapter.

**3.3 What the posts measure, and what they only mention.** Technical and customer become checkable requirements (years, languages). Product appears as a duty in every lab post (OpenAI "Share field feedback"; Anthropic "codify repeatable deployment patterns") but in no requirement list; judgment appears as adjectives ("Model calm and judgment"). Exception: Anthropic Staff post asks for track records. Author's reading, labelled.

**3.4 The sceptics.** Pragmatic Engineer two disappointed hires, "IT services mindset", "I quit 4 weeks later" (T4). Gayle: "a lot of content labeled FDE is really AI engineering under a different name"; boundary by ownership not skills (T4). roadmap.sh 97 nodes, tools-heavy, no employer source (T4). Map use: lopsided profile tells you which mislabelled job you are in or heading to.

**3.5 The rubric** (threat-grid, 16 rows: domain · level / behaviour / evidence that counts).

**3.6 Placing yourself.** Rules: done not known; one instance is a data point, a level is repeated; artefact + witness; lowest sustained; uneven is normal. Prior-role starting points (Bloomberry prior roles T2; the gap mapping is the author's inference).

**3.7 Template.** Self-assessment blank, then filled for the composite's mid-level FDE at month nine (composite callout; ranges only; consistent with composite-arc Ch 4–13).

**3.8 What the map can't tell you.** No predictive validity; levels ≠ titles across employers; reused in Getting Hired and Levels chapters (prose, unlinked). Closing tip.
