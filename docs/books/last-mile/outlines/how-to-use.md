# How to Use This Book (`how-to-use`)

- **Part / week line:** Part I · The Role / 第 I 部分 · 这个角色
- **phase-num:** H
- **Length:** 2,000–2,800 words
- **Writer:** the orchestrator, by hand

## p.goal draft
> Most of what is written about Forward Deployed Engineers is confident and unsourced, so this book grades every claim and tells you how far to trust it. Use it by situation: find what is going wrong, go to that page, and take the template.

(The first sentence is 23 words.)

## STEPs

**H.1 Who this is for, and three paths**
- **Want the job:** Part I, the skill map, then Part IV.
- **Doing the job:** the Part II chapter for your current phase.
- **Something has gone wrong:** the situation index (H.5), or When Engagements Fail.

What the book is not:
- not interview prep (we link to that);
- not a course on the AI stack (the rest of this wiki is);
- not a history of Palantir.

**H.2 How evidence is graded**
Tiers T1–T4, with one real example each:
- **T1:** The Markup's testing of NYC MyCity.
- **T2:** Palantir's S-1 phase economics.
- **T3:** The Register on NHS FDP adoption.
- **T4:** an OpenAI job post.

Two further points:
- Job posts are T4 evidence of how the work is done, but primary evidence of what employers ask for.
- An honest admission: T1 evidence on how engagements run barely exists. Of our ten best cases, the only one with a T1-measured outcome is a failure.

**H.3 Echoes**
- Define the term, then give two examples:
  - Indeed growth figures that trace to one source;
  - Morgan Stanley's "98%", which drifted from team adoption to daily use.
- Rule: we name an echo where we find one.

**H.4 Composites and Verified boxes**
- Why composites: nobody publishes the messy middle of an engagement.
- The rules: ranges only, never a real company, and every beat cites the pattern it dramatises.
- Show a live composite box introducing the insurer.
- Show a live Verified box, e.g. the O'Reilly book's Early Release status, due 2027-12-25, or the Chinese-term note.
- Verified boxes date-stamp anything perishable.

**H.5 The situation index**
- A `.threat-grid` with columns: situation / go to / why.
- 28 rows from bible §8.
- Link only published chapters (how-to-use, before-the-engagement, discovery, scoping-and-the-first-win). Name the rest in prose with "(coming)" / "（即将发布）".

**H.6 A note on the term**
- 前线部署工程师, with its aliases.
- English readers: the Chinese market is covered in Part IV.
- Chinese readers: the US data is graded the same way.
