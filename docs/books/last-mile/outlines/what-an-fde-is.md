# Ch 1: What an FDE Is, and Isn't (`what-an-fde-is`)

Part I · The Role / 第 I 部分 · 角色 · phase-num 1 · 3,500–4,500 words.

## p.goal draft
> Take a job titled FDE and you may spend it demoing, configuring or reselling, because the title now covers several jobs. The test is ownership: discovery through handoff, with code as the means.

## Argument (bible §5)
An FDE is defined by owning the outcome across discovery through handoff, with the code as the means.
Every title that drops one of those stages is a different job, whatever it is called.

## STEPs
**1.1 The definition, from the people who hire.** OpenAI SF post: "lead complex end-to-end deployments", "own discovery, technical scoping, system design, build, and production rollout", success = adoption, workflow impact, eval-driven feedback (T4, as what employers ask for). Anthropic Staff FDE: "lead, not only to implement" (T4). The healthcare line: owns the technical solution; commercial or executive relationship "not required" (T4) — outcome ownership is not account ownership.

**1.2 Five stages and a loop; the code is the means.** Discovery, scope, build, production, handoff, plus the product loop. What "own" means at each: a decision right, not attendance. Salesforce: coding is "table stakes" (T2). Palantir "startup CTO" / Decagon "founding CTO" — flag as an echo of one self-description (T4).

**1.3 One title, several jobs.** Bloomberry 60/30/10 (T2; one analyst's classification, method unstated). Anthropic's "Applied AI Engineer, Enterprise": the partner builds (T4). China: Tencent files the role under product, ByteDance under R&D, its FDE interns under Sales–Sales Support (T3, iFanr). The job family tells you what the employer thinks it bought.

**1.4 The neighbours, by what they own.** Tryolabs consultant/contractor test (T4). Template 1: role-comparison grid (FDE / SA / SE / consultant / applied AI engineer): owns · drops · who catches it. Book's own synthesis (opinion).

**1.5 The sceptics are partly right.** Gayle: "a lot of content labeled FDE is really AI engineering under a different name" (T4). Pragmatic Engineer: "typical IT services mindset", "I quit 4 weeks later" (T4, two people). Betts "maybe 10% of the market" (T4 via WSJ via PE). Labs moving deployment into separate companies (Verified box: OpenAI Deployment Company, Ode). What the ownership test predicts about each complaint.

**1.6 Composite: who owned what at the insurer.** Locked facts only (free 3-month PoC → 30-day paid pilot; chatbot → claims triage; propose-only; handoff drill). Which neighbour would have dropped which stage.

**1.7 Template: "Is this an FDE job?" checklist.** Blank, then filled for Harvey's Forward Deployed Finance Engineer, Buyout (posted 2026-10-06). Verdict: owns the outcome, but the means is workflow-building not code; pre-sales heavy. Pay in a Verified box.
