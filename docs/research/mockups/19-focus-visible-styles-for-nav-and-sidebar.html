WCAG 2.1 SC 2.4.11 (Focus Appearance) requires a visible focus indicator for keyboard users. The site already applies a custom box-shadow focus ring to .site-search-trigger:focus-visible (site.css line 148) and the .skip-link (line 128), but nav links, sidebar links, and the lang switcher rely on the browser default, which is often invisible against the parchment background. The six-line addition above closes this gap consistently.
